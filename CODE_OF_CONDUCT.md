# Community code of conduct

We want project spaces to be useful, welcoming, and safe for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code without attacking people.
- Give specific, actionable feedback and assume good faith while still addressing real risks.
- Respect privacy, licensing, attribution, and project boundaries.
- Make room for questions and corrections without ridicule.
- Disclose relevant conflicts of interest and security concerns responsibly.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate disruption, spam, impersonation, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions and restrict participation when necessary to protect the community or the project. Serious concerns can be reported privately through the security contact described in `SECURITY.md`. Reports will be reviewed in context, with privacy respected as far as reasonably possible.
